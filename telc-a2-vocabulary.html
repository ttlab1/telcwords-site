TELC A2 German Vocabulary List: 1,049 Words with English

At telc Deutsch A2 you move from single phrases to short connected texts: describing your day, talking about the past, making plans and handling routine tasks like appointments, shopping and travel. The vocabulary grows into work, leisure, health and services.

Below is the full A2 word list used in TELC Words, with English meanings and the article for every noun. Search it, scan it by letter, then play it as a quick-fire game with a timer, streaks and bonus rounds to make the words stick.

What is in the TELC A2 list

The list contains 1,049 entries: about 620 nouns with article, 229 verbs and 116 adjectives, plus connecting words, expressions and phrases. Typical topics:

  • work, school and daily routine
  • travel, accommodation and transport
  • health, doctor visits and the body
  • clothes, shopping and prices
  • plans, invitations and simple opinions

In German, a list like this is called a Wortliste or Wortschatzliste. This page is the TELC Words A2 Wortliste, with the article and an English meaning for every word.

How to learn the A2 words faster

  • Learn verbs with a short example, such as "einen Termin machen" instead of only "machen".
  • Many new A2 verbs are separable, such as abholen, einsteigen and herunterladen. The separable verbs list groups all of them by prefix and explains the word order.
  • Group words by topic (travel, health, work). Related words are easier to recall together.
  • Use the streak mechanic: aim for answers without hesitation. Fast recall is what helps in the speaking part.
  • Mix in A1 words from time to time to keep the basics sharp.

Colour code used on this page and in the game: der masculine, die feminine, das neuter.

TELC A2 vocabulary: frequently asked questions

What is the difference between A1 and A2 vocabulary?

A1 covers survival German: greetings, numbers, food, family. A2 adds routine situations such as work, health, travel and plans, with more verbs and connecting words.
