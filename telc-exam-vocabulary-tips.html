How to Learn TELC Vocabulary Effectively

Vocabulary is the part of exam preparation you can improve fastest. This plan works for any telc level from A1 to C1 and takes about fifteen minutes a day.

▶ Start a session

1. Pick the right level

Choose the level of your exam: A1, A2, B1, B2 or C1. If a level feels too easy, move up. If you keep losing hearts, step down and rebuild the base.

2. Learn nouns with their article

Gender is part of the word. Read the guide to der, die, das and use the colour code in the game.

3. Practise in short daily sessions

A block of ten words takes about two minutes. Do a few blocks a day. Regular repetition moves words into long-term memory much better than a single long session.

4. Review your mistakes

Words you answer wrongly return in the bonus round. Those are the words worth your time, so do not skip it.

5. Learn phrases, not only words

From B1 upward, many points depend on verbs with prepositions and fixed expressions. Learn "sich bewerben um" as a unit, with its case and preposition.

6. Use the words

Write one sentence for every few new words and say it aloud. Active use fixes a word better than recognition alone.

Frequently asked questions

How long should I study vocabulary each day?

Ten to twenty focused minutes a day usually beats a long weekly session, because frequent review keeps words in long-term memory.

Should I learn words by level or by topic?

Start with your exam level, then group words by topic when reviewing. Topics give context, which helps recall.
