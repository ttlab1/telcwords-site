TELC A1 vocabulary

The telc Deutsch A1 exam checks that you can handle simple, everyday situations in German: introducing yourself, talking about family, ordering food, asking for directions and filling in short forms. The vocabulary is concrete and high-frequency, so a solid word base is the fastest route to a pass.

TELC A2 vocabulary

At telc Deutsch A2 you move from single phrases to short connected texts: describing your day, talking about the past, making plans and handling routine tasks like appointments, shopping and travel. The vocabulary grows into work, leisure, health and services.

TELC B1 vocabulary

telc Deutsch B1 is a common target for integration courses, work and residence purposes. You are expected to follow clear standard German on familiar topics, write personal and semi-formal letters and take part in a spoken exam about everyday and work topics.

▶ Play B1   See the full 2,098-word B1 list →

TELC B2 vocabulary

At telc Deutsch B2 you are expected to understand complex texts, argue a position and write structured, formal texts. Vocabulary shifts toward abstract topics, nominal style and fixed verb-noun combinations (Funktionsverbgefüge) common in newspapers, reports and professional communication.

▶ Play B2   See the full 714-word B2 list →

TELC C1 vocabulary

telc Deutsch C1 tests advanced, near-fluent German: understanding demanding texts, expressing yourself spontaneously and using precise, formal and academic language. Vocabulary is organised around themes such as studying, mobility, technology, health, society and the environment, with many fixed phrases and verb-preposition combinations.

How TELC Words works

  1. See a German word and pick the correct English meaning from six options.
  2. Beat the 10-second timer. You have three hearts; fast, correct answers build streaks up to ×4.
  3. Finish blocks of 10 words and earn XP and a bonus round.
  4. Words you miss come back in the bonus round, so weak words get extra practice.

Nouns are colour-coded by article, so you learn der die das together with the word.

Frequently asked questions

What is TELC Words?

TELC Words is a free browser game that trains German vocabulary for telc exams from A1 to C1. You answer against a timer, build streaks, and revisit the words you got wrong.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your progress and personal bests are stored in your own browser.

Which levels are covered?

A1, A2, B1, B2 and C1, with 6,224 words in total.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The game is designed mobile-first and works in any modern browser.

Can I hear the pronunciation?

Yes. Tap the speaker button on a word to hear it read aloud in German. Voice quality depends on the German voices installed on your device.
