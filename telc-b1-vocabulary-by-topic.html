TELC B1 Vocabulary by Topic: 636 German Words with English

This page sorts 636 words from the B1 list of TELC Words into 10 everyday topics, such as work, housing, health and travel. Every word has its English meaning, and every noun has its article in colour. A short note before each table tells you where the words are used and which ones to learn together.

The full B1 vocabulary list has all 2,098 words in alphabetical order. This page is a smaller, themed selection from that list for revision. Pick one topic, read the note, cover the English column and test yourself. Then press the play button under the topic: the game starts with only the words of that topic, so you can check what you really remember.

Work and job applications (60 words)

These words come up when you read a job advert, write an application, talk to colleagues or ask about your pay. Learn the pairs together: Arbeitgeber and Arbeitnehmer, einstellen and entlassen, Lohn and Gehalt.

Learn sich bewerben as a full unit with its prepositions: sich um eine Stelle bewerben (for a job) and sich bei einer Firma bewerben (at a company). The noun that goes with it is die Bewerbung.

Ich möchte mich um die Stelle bewerben.

Housing and home (64 words)

Use these words when you look for a flat, read a rental advert, talk to the caretaker or describe where you live. Learn mieten (to rent from someone) together with vermieten (to rent out), and die Miete together with die Wohnung.

Floors are counted with der Stock: the ground floor is das Erdgeschoss. Many household verbs are separable, like aufräumen and einziehen: in a normal sentence, the first part moves to the end.

Die Wohnung liegt im zweiten Stock.

Die Miete ist sehr teuer.

Health and the body (70 words)

You need these words at the doctor's, at the pharmacy, when you call in sick or when you explain what hurts. Learn body parts with their article, because you will often say mein or meine in front of them.

A very useful pattern is weh tun: the body part is the subject of the sentence. Learn the polite wish Gute Besserung, and learn sich erkälten and sich erholen with sich.

Mein Rücken tut weh.

Ich habe Fieber und Husten.

Food, shopping and money (72 words)

These words help you in the supermarket, in a restaurant, at the bank and when you compare prices. Group them by place: what you buy at die Bäckerei or die Metzgerei, what you see on die Rechnung, what you do with das Konto.

Learn the money verbs as a family: bezahlen, einzahlen, überweisen. Learn opposites in pairs: billig and teuer, sparen and ausgeben.

Ich möchte bar bezahlen.

Die Rechnung, bitte.

Travel and transport (68 words)

These words appear on station boards, in travel announcements, at the hotel reception and on the road. Many travel verbs are separable: einsteigen, aussteigen, umsteigen, abfahren. Practise them in short sentences, not only as single words.

Learn die Abfahrt and die Ankunft as a pair. For delays, learn the whole expression Verspätung haben, and the verb sich verspäten.

Der Zug hat zehn Minuten Verspätung.

Wir müssen in Köln umsteigen.

Authorities, offices and documents (60 words)

These are the words of forms, letters from offices, the post office and the police. You will meet them when you register an address, apply for something, sign a contract or send an important letter.

Learn typical word pairs as one unit: ein Formular ausfüllen, einen Antrag stellen, einen Vertrag unterschreiben. Personal details on forms often use ledig, verheiratet and verwitwet, so learn those three together.

Bitte füllen Sie das Formular aus.

Education and training (57 words)

These words describe school, courses, vocational training and university. They are useful when you talk about your own education, read a course description or explain what you want to learn next.

Learn the word families together: lernen and studieren, der Lehrer and der Schüler, die Ausbildung and der Auszubildende. Also learn the verb that goes with an exam: eine Prüfung machen or eine Prüfung bestehen.

Ich mache eine Ausbildung.

Media and technology (56 words)

These words cover newspapers, television, radio, phones and machines at home or at work. You need them to talk about the news, describe a device that is kaputt or follow a Gebrauchsanweisung.

Learn opposite verbs in pairs: einschalten and ausschalten. Notice that die Nachricht is one message, while die Nachrichten is also the word for the news on TV or radio.

Ich lese jeden Tag die Zeitung.

Environment, nature and weather (62 words)

These words help you talk about the weather, the landscape, animals and plants, and about protecting the environment. They are common in weather reports, in travel descriptions and in discussions about energy and waste.

Weather verbs use es as the subject: es regnet, es schneit. Learn nouns and verbs together: der Regen and regnen, der Schnee and schneien. For environment topics, die Umwelt schützen and Energie verbrauchen are good phrases to learn as a whole.

Morgen regnet es.

Im Winter ist es kalt.

Feelings, opinions and relationships (67 words)

These words let you say how you feel, give your opinion, agree or disagree, and talk about family, friends and partners. They are useful in almost every conversation and in personal letters.

Many of these verbs are reflexive and take a preposition, so learn them as phrases: sich freuen auf (something in the future), sich freuen über (something that is already here), sich beschweren über, sich verlieben in. For opinions, learn zustimmen, überzeugen and die Meinung together.

Ich freue mich auf den Urlaub.

How to study vocabulary by topic

Learning by topic works because words that belong together are easier to remember together. When you think about a visit to the doctor, words like Termin, Rezept and Apotheke come as a group. Here is a simple routine you can use with any topic on this page.

  1. One topic per session. Choose one topic and give it 15 to 20 minutes. Do not try to learn several topics in one evening.
  2. Nouns with articles. Say every noun aloud with its article, for example die Wohnung, der Schlüssel, das Zimmer. The colour helps your memory. Our der, die, das guide explains useful patterns.
  3. Verbs in a sentence. Do not learn a verb alone. Write one short sentence about your own life with it. A sentence about you is easier to remember than a sentence from a book.
  4. Cover and test. Cover the English column and say the meaning. Then cover the German column and try the other direction. Mark the words you miss.
  5. Speak about the topic. Try to talk for one minute about the topic, using as many words from the table as you can. Record yourself on your phone if you like.
  6. Repeat with spaces. Come back to the same topic after one day, after three days and after a week. Short reviews beat one long session.

For a full study plan, read how to learn TELC vocabulary. To check words quickly and often, use the B1 game: it repeats the words you get wrong in a bonus round.

TELC B1 vocabulary by topic: frequently asked questions

Is this the official telc word list?

No. TELC Words is an independent practice tool and is not connected to telc gGmbH. The words on this page come from the B1 list used in the TELC Words game. It is a learning aid, not an official exam document.

Does this page include every B1 word?

No. This page is a themed selection of 636 words from the 2,098-word B1 list in TELC Words. Grammar words, small connecting words and words that do not fit one clear topic are not included here. You can find all of them on the full alphabetical B1 page.

Why does each word appear in only one topic?

To keep the page short and easy to use, every word is placed in the topic where it fits best. Many words are useful in more than one situation, so feel free to use them in other topics too.

How can I practise these words?

Read one topic, test yourself by covering one column, then press the play button under the topic. The TELC Words game then asks only the words of that topic. You can also choose a B1 topic on the home screen of the game. It is free, needs no sign-up and works on your phone.
