TELC A1 German Vocabulary List: 1,099 Words with English

The telc Deutsch A1 exam checks that you can handle simple, everyday situations in German: introducing yourself, talking about family, ordering food, asking for directions and filling in short forms. The vocabulary is concrete and high-frequency, so a solid word base is the fastest route to a pass.

Below is the full A1 word list used in TELC Words, with English meanings and the article for every noun. Search it, scan it by letter, then play it as a quick-fire game with a timer, streaks and bonus rounds to make the words stick.

What is in the TELC A1 list

The list contains 1,099 entries: about 628 nouns with article, 158 verbs and 116 adjectives, plus connecting words, expressions and phrases. Typical topics:

  • greetings, personal details and family
  • numbers, dates, times and days of the week
  • food, drinks and shopping
  • housing, directions and public transport
  • health, body parts and simple appointments

How to learn the A1 words faster

  • Learn every noun together with its article (der, die, das). At A1 the article is part of the word.
  • Practise in short daily sessions of 10 to 15 minutes instead of one long weekly session.
  • Say each word out loud. Speaking is part of the exam, and pronunciation sticks better when you use it.
  • Revisit the words you got wrong. TELC Words keeps those in a bonus round so weak words come back.

Colour code used on this page and in the game: der masculine, die feminine, das neuter.

How many words do I need for the telc A1 exam?

There is no single official number. This page lists all 1,099 A1 entries used in TELC Words so you can see exactly what you are practising.

Is this the official telc word list?

TELC Words is an independent practice tool. Its lists are compiled from TELC-style vocabulary material and are meant for practice, not as an official exam document.

Do I need to learn the articles for A1?

Yes. German nouns are tested with their gender, and a wrong article is a common source of lost points. Every noun in the trainer is colour-coded: blue for der, pink for die, green for das.
