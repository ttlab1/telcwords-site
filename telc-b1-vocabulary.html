TELC B1 German Vocabulary List: 2,098 Words with English

telc Deutsch B1 is a common target for integration courses, work and residence purposes. You are expected to follow clear standard German on familiar topics, write personal and semi-formal letters and take part in a spoken exam about everyday and work topics.

Below is the full B1 word list used in TELC Words, with English meanings and the article for every noun. Search it, scan it by letter, then play it as a quick-fire game with a timer, streaks and bonus rounds to make the words stick.

What is in the TELC B1 list

The list contains 2,098 entries: about 1,053 nouns with article, 489 verbs and 125 adjectives, plus connecting words, expressions and phrases. Typical topics:

  • work, applications and the job market
  • education, training and courses
  • media, technology and the internet
  • environment, society and rules
  • feelings, opinions and complaints

How to learn the B1 words faster

  • Learn separable and reflexive verbs as full phrases: "sich bewerben um", "anrufen", "sich freuen auf".
  • For the Sprachbausteine and letter-writing parts, connecting words (obwohl, deshalb, trotzdem) earn many points.
  • Use bonus rounds to re-test the words you missed. Spaced repetition beats rereading a list.
  • Keep a streak going: short, frequent practice is more effective than cramming the week before the exam.

Colour code used on this page and in the game: der masculine, die feminine, das neuter.
