TELC B2 German Vocabulary List: 714 Words with English

At telc Deutsch B2 you are expected to understand complex texts, argue a position and write structured, formal texts. Vocabulary shifts toward abstract topics, nominal style and fixed verb-noun combinations (Funktionsverbgefüge) common in newspapers, reports and professional communication.

Below is the full B2 word list used in TELC Words, with English meanings and the article for every noun. Search it, scan it by letter, then play it as a quick-fire game with a timer, streaks and bonus rounds to make the words stick.

What is in the TELC B2 list

The list contains 714 entries: about 393 nouns with article, 207 verbs and 36 adjectives, plus connecting words, expressions and phrases. Typical topics:

  • work, careers and the economy
  • society, politics and the law
  • science, health and technology
  • formal letters, complaints and requests
  • fixed expressions and verb-noun combinations

How to learn the B2 words faster

  • Learn collocations, not isolated words: "Verantwortung übernehmen", "in Betracht ziehen", "zur Verfügung stehen".
  • Read short news articles and highlight words from this list when you meet them in context.
  • Watch the prepositions that go with verbs and nouns. They are tested constantly and are easy to forget.
  • Practise little and often so the long list stays active in memory.

Colour code used on this page and in the game: der masculine, die feminine, das neuter.
