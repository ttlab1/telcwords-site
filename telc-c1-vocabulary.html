TELC C1 German Vocabulary List: 1,264 Words with English

telc Deutsch C1 tests advanced, near-fluent German: understanding demanding texts, expressing yourself spontaneously and using precise, formal and academic language. Vocabulary is organised around themes such as studying, mobility, technology, health, society and the environment, with many fixed phrases and verb-preposition combinations.

What is in the TELC C1 list

The list contains 1,264 entries: about 449 nouns with article, 582 verbs and 143 adjectives, plus connecting words, expressions and phrases. Typical topics:

  • university studies and academic life
  • mobility, technology and artificial intelligence
  • health, nutrition and the workplace
  • migration, identity and society
  • environment, sustainability and economics

In German, a list like this is called a Wortliste or Wortschatzliste. This page is the TELC Words C1 Wortliste, with the article and an English meaning for every word.

How to learn the C1 words faster

  • Learn verb phrases with their case and preposition, for example "sich beschäftigen mit (+ Dat.)".
  • Use the words actively: write two sentences for each new phrase.
  • Study by theme. C1 texts stay on one topic for a long time, so topic vocabulary pays off.
  • Return to wrong answers often. At this level the difficult words are the ones you almost know.

Colour code used on this page and in the game: der masculine, die feminine, das neuter.
